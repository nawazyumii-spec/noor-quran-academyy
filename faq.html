Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about online Quran classes, courses and enrollment.

Are classes one-to-one?

Yes. The academy is structured around individual student-teacher lessons.

Do you teach kids and adults?

Yes. Courses can be selected according to age, current level and learning goal.

Which courses are available?

Noorani Qaida, Quran Reading, Tajweed, Hifz-ul-Quran, Islamic Studies and Arabic Basics.

Can I choose a convenient class time?

Scheduling is discussed with the academy based on availability and the student's routine.

How do I book a trial?

Use the Free Trial page and submit the student's details. The form prepares an email to onlinequran@gmail.com.

Is the fee shown on the website final?

No. The fee-plan page shows lesson-frequency options. Contact the academy for current pricing and availability.
