About Noor Quran Academy

A focused online learning environment for students who want to read, recite, memorize and learn about the Quran with structure and personal attention.

Our approach

Noor Quran Academy is built around live, one-to-one online learning. The goal is to make Quran education organized, approachable and consistent for both children and adults.

Every student's starting level is different. Course content and lesson focus can be adjusted around the student's needs and learning goal.

What students can work on

  • Arabic letters and Noorani Qaida foundations
  • Fluent Quran reading
  • Tajweed and pronunciation practice
  • Hifz-ul-Quran with revision
  • Islamic Studies and everyday learning
  • Basic Arabic support for Quran learning

Learning environment

Lessons are designed to be respectful, calm and focused. Students receive direct teacher attention, opportunities to practice and guidance on what to revise between lessons.
